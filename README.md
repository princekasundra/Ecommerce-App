How It Works
1.Login: Enter email and password to access the app. The app remembers your login for next time.
2.Home Screen: View products, search by name, or click a product to see details. Press the back button to log out with a confirmation.
3.Cart: Add items from the product details screen. Use icons to adjust quantities or remove items.
4.Checkout: Proceed to checkout from the cart to complete your purchase.
